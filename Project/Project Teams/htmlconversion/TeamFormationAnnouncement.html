What counts as a complete team

A project team is considered fully formed when all four of the following conditions are met:

  1. The team has exactly four members.
  2. Each of the four members has independently submitted the team preference survey.
  3. Each member's submission identifies the same four people — that is, every member has listed the other three teammates by name and student ID.

A team where only some members have submitted, or where the submitted names do not all agree, is not yet complete and will not be recognized as a team assignment.

Teams formed so far

The teams that meet all of the above criteria as of today have been posted on the course website.

to complete the process. To do so:

  1. Coordinate with your three intended teammates and agree on your group.
  2. Each of you must submit the team preference survey — one submission per person, not one submission on behalf of the group.
  3. In your submission, list your own name and student ID, then the names and student IDs of your three teammates.
  4. All four submissions must identify the same four people.
