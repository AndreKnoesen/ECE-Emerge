EEC1 Final Project: Role Declaration

To: EEC1 Spring 2026
From: Andre Knoesen
Date: May 12, 2026
Subject: Declaring your team's role assignments — deadline Friday, May 22 at noon

Your project teams are now finalized and posted on the course website. The next step is for each team to assign its four specialist roles.

Why we are declaring roles

The digital scale project naturally divides into four areas of specialist focus: the analog signal chain, the M2K–MATLAB interface and GUI, calibration and performance analysis, and system integration and reporting. Within a team, every member contributes to every area — but each member also takes one of these four roles as their point of deepest ownership: the area where they go furthest, the area they speak for at check-offs and in the report, and the area where the rest of the team turns to them first.

The role is about ownership and expertise, not exclusion. The role holder is the team's designated integrator for that area, not its gatekeeper.

Three-member teams take three of the four roles and distribute the System Integration responsibilities as described in the project document.

Read this first

The four roles, what each one owns, which check-off each one leads, and which report section each one authors are described in detail here:

Project Roles — full descriptions

Read that section together as a team before you decide. The descriptions are specific: each role names the lab work it builds on (Role 1 builds on Lab 8, Role 2 builds on Lab 6 Section 4.2), the report section it owns, and the check-off where it takes the lead. Picking based on the headings alone will get you a poor fit.

How to choose roles within your team

  1. Read all four role descriptions individually first. Then come together as a team to discuss.
  2. Match interests and strengths to roles, not the other way around. A teammate who enjoyed Lab 8's signal-chain bring-up is a natural fit for Role 1; a teammate who wrote clean App Designer code in MP5 is a natural fit for Role 2; a teammate who is meticulous with calibration plots and statistics is a natural fit for Role 3; a teammate who keeps the team on schedule and writes clearly is a natural fit for Role 4.
  3. No role is more important than another. All four carry equal weight in the project's scope and in grading.
  4. For three-member teams: decide which two of the remaining three teammates will absorb the System Integration responsibilities (typically the Calibration & Performance lead does most of this, but agree on it explicitly).
  5. Reach consensus before submitting. The submission is one form per team, not one per member — disagreement within a team blocks the submission.

What you will submit

One submission per team. The form will collect:

  • Your team number (as posted on the course teams page).
  • For each team member: their name and which of the four roles they will hold.
  • For three-member teams: a brief note on how the integration responsibilities are distributed.

The submission process (form link and any account requirements) will be announced separately before the deadline.

Deadline

Friday, May 22, 2026 at 12:00 PM (noon).

After the deadline, role assignments are recorded and used to identify each role's POC for TA workshops, check-off coordination, and report submissions. Changing roles after the declaration is disruptive — choose carefully.

Next Steps

  1. Read the Project Roles section of the project document with your teammates.
  2. Discuss role fit openly; pick interests and strengths over convenience.
  3. Reach agreement as a team.
  4. Watch for the submission form (announced separately).
  5. Submit one role declaration per team by Friday, May 22 at noon.
