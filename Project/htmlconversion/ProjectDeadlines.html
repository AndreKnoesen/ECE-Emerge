Capstone Phase Deadlines: Lab 8 and Final Project

EEC1 Spring 2026

This page consolidates every project deadline from Lab 8 (start of the team-project stage) through the Final Exam. It is the authoritative single-page reference for the capstone phase. Pre-project lab work (Labs 1 through 7) is tracked in the course schedule, not here. If a date listed here conflicts with the course schedule or the project document, that is a bug; report it.

The team-project stage begins with Module 8 (Lab 8). From the Lab 8 prelab onward, almost every deliverable is a team submission. The exceptions are bracketed at each end: the Reflective AI Exercise inside the Lab 8 prelab is individual, and two individual elements close the project (Individual Exit Survey, Final Exam).

Times are Pacific. "Noon" means 12:00 PM.

Week of May 18: Lab 8, where the team project starts

Module 8 is where capstone team work begins. Almost every deliverable in this week is a team submission. The one individual element is the Reflective AI Exercise inside the prelab; each member writes and submits it on their own. Everything else, from the Team Coordination Plan through the Phase 1 Milestone Report, is one submission per team.

Stage 5 sign-off paths.

  2. Hardware-only release. Hardware passes TA verification but MATLAB is not yet running: hardware-prototype kit is released anyway so soldering can begin; MATLAB sign-off is still required separately for full Deliverable #5 credit.

Individual elements at the end of the project

After weeks of team work, the project closes with two individual elements. These are how each member's personal contribution and learning is assessed; team submissions do not substitute.

  • Individual Exit Survey. Each member describes their personal contributions to the project and evaluates each teammate. General statements such as "we all participated equally" will not be credited. Documented in the project document under Deliverables and Grading. Due Thursday, June 4 at 5:00 PM, same deadline as the five Final Report sections.
  • Final Exam. Wed, Jun 10, 8:00–10:00 AM, Max Kleiber Hall 3 (to be confirmed).

What is NOT a deadline (but matters)

These are not graded due dates, but missing them creates downstream pain.

  • Load cell 48-hour return. Whenever your team checks out a load cell for off-lab work, the return time is recorded at checkout. Late return: point penalty, and another team may be blocked.
  • Front-load the soldering. As soon as the breadboard signals are clean, transition to the soldered prototype. Calibration data taken on a breadboard is not reproducible. Teams that delay soldering consistently run out of bring-up time before Check-Off 2.
  • Do not break down the breadboard until Check-Off 2 passes. The breadboard is a parallel test platform while the soldered prototype is under construction.
  • First INA125 return. Recover the first INA125 from the breadboard after Check-Off 2 and return it with a log entry. No log, no equipment-portion credit.
