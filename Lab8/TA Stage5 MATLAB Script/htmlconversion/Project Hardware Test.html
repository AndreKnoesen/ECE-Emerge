TA_HW_Test.m — Lab 8 Stage 5 Hardware Test

Purpose

TA_HW_Test.m is a TA-facing MATLAB script for verifying that a fully assembled scale circuit (load cell → INA125 instrumentation amplifier → output stage) behaves correctly under known loads.

The script uses the ADALM2000 (M2K) to:

  1. Power the circuit from the M2K's V+ / V- supplies (±5 V).
  2. Capture 1 second of data from two analog inputs at three known weights: 0 g, 250 g, and 500 g.
  3. Plot the INA output voltage and the final circuit output voltage versus time across all three weight intervals.
  4. Report the mean and standard deviation of the final output voltage at each weight.

Required Files

Download both scripts: TA_HW_Test.zip (contains TA_HW_Test.m and M2K.m). Unzip into a single folder.

Both files must be in the same folder (or on the MATLAB path):

Required Files
File Role
TA_HW_Test.m The test script (run this).
M2K.m Class wrapper around the libm2k API used by the script (M2K connection, calibration, channel/supply control, sample acquisition).

The libm2k MATLAB bindings must already be installed and on the MATLAB path. See the references inside M2K.m:
- libm2k API: https://analogdevicesinc.github.io/libm2k/index.html
- ADALM-2000 with MATLAB: https://wiki.analog.com/university/tools/m2k/matlab

Hardware Connections

Wire the M2K to the assembled scale circuit as follows:

Hardware Connections
M2K Pin Connects To
V+ Circuit V+ rail
V- Circuit V- rail
GND Circuit ground
CH1 (1+, 1-) Final output of the circuit (Vout)
CH2 (2+, 2-) INA125 output (pin 10 or pin 11 of the INA, VINA)

The script drives V+ to +5 V and V- to -5 V via the M2K power supplies, so no external bench supply is required.

How to Run

  1. Open MATLAB in the folder containing TA_HW_Test.m and M2K.m.
  2. With the circuit disconnected from the M2K, type TA_HW_Test at the MATLAB prompt and press Enter.
  3. Follow the prompts in the Command Window — the script will pause and wait for you to press Enter at each step:
How to Run
Step Prompt What to do
1 Disconnect circuit from M2K. → Press Enter to calibrate Make sure the circuit is disconnected, then press Enter. The script creates the M2K object and calibrates the instrument.
2 Connect circuit with M2K. → Press Enter to initiate M2K and measure 0g Connect the circuit per the table above (no weight on the scale), then press Enter. The script enables CH1, CH2, V+, V-, sets the ADC rate to 100 kHz, and captures 0 g.
3 Put 250g on the scale. → Press Enter to measure Place the 250 g mass on the scale, wait for it to settle, then press Enter. The script captures 250 g.
4 Put 500g on the scale. → Press Enter to measure Replace with the 500 g mass (or add the second 250 g), wait for it to settle, then press Enter. The script captures 500 g.

Each capture is 1 second of samples acquired at 100 kHz on both CH1 and CH2.

Outputs

1. Combined voltage plot

After the third measurement, the script opens a single figure plotting both channels across the three weight intervals stitched together on the time axis (0 g over 0–1 s, 250 g over 1–2 s, 500 g over 2–3 s):

  • Ch2 — VINA — output of the INA125 stage
  • Ch1 — Vout — final circuit output

You should see two clearly separated step changes in both traces as the weight is increased from 0 g → 250 g → 500 g. The two traces should track each other (offset and/or scaled, depending on the post-INA stage gain — in the example figure above, the post-INA stage inverts and amplifies the signal, so Vout steps downward as VINA steps upward).

2. Mean and standard deviation in the Command Window

The script prints two 1×3 arrays for the final output (CH1) at each weight:

```
Mean (in V) for 0g, 250g and 500g:

Standard deviation (in V) for 0g, 250g and 500g:

```

Use the means to check that the circuit's transfer characteristic at 0 / 250 / 500 g matches the design target. Use the standard deviations as a quick noise/stability check — a clean circuit should show small standard deviations (well under the step size between weight levels).

Troubleshooting

  • "m2k object is null" — MATLAB cannot see the M2K. Replug the USB cable, restart MATLAB, and confirm the libm2k MATLAB bindings are on the path.
  • Flat lines on both channels — check V+ / V- / GND wiring and that the circuit is actually connected to CH1 / CH2 after the "Connect circuit with M2K" prompt.
  • CH2 (INA) steps but CH1 (Vout) does not — issue is in the post-INA stage, not the INA itself.
  • Both channels noisy / high standard deviation — check ground connections, the load cell wiring, and that the scale platform is mechanically stable during the 1 s capture window.
